# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in HalfRand a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality,
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, give constructive feedback, accept responsibility, and focus on what is
best for the community. Harassment, insults, discrimination, sexualized attention,
threats, and publishing private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project owner through the contact method
on their GitHub profile. Maintainers will review reports fairly, protect reporters'
privacy where possible, and may warn, temporarily ban, or permanently exclude anyone
whose behavior harms the community.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents HalfRand.
It is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
